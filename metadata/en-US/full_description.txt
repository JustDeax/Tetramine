<p>Classic arcade puzzle game where you have to stack falling tetromino — 4-block pieces, create solid lines out of them and play for the score.</p>

<p>The original mechanics are retained here, with smooth controls, but with a modern, minimalist design.</p>

<b>Features:</b>
<ul>
  <li>★ Modern Material Design 3 And ViewModel</li>
  <li>★ Comfortable control setup</li>
  <li>★ Fair distribution of "7-bag" figures</li>
  <li>★ Hard drop and soft drop</li>
  <li>★ Cool Rotation System</li>
  <li>★ Advanced Scoring System</li>
  <li>★ Ghost figure</li>
  <li>★ Full customization of controls and appearance</li>
  <li>★ Dynamic and AMOLED Theme support</li>
  <li>★ Detailed statistics</li>
